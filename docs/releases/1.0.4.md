Two fixes to focusing a note on the built-in graph.

## The focus ring now zooms with the graph

The ring drawn around a focused note was positioned using the graph's zoom and
pan, but its **radius ignored the zoom** entirely. Zooming in made the node grow
while the ring stayed the same number of pixels, so the ring ended up inside the
node; zooming out left it floating well outside.

The radius is now scaled exactly like the position, with the small gap between
node and ring kept constant — that gap is breathing space, not part of the node.

## The rest of the graph recedes further

The dimming went down again, because the focused route still had to compete with
its surroundings:

| | 1.0.2 | 1.0.3 | **1.0.4** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Notes outside the focus set | 18% | 6% | **3%** |
| Edges outside the route | 20% | 5% | **2%** |

Nothing else changed.
